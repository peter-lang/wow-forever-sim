What gear stats are worth

A range's number averages each stat's XP-an-hour gain over its levels, then converts. The tables say how much of a stat is worth as much as 10 strength (melee) or 10 spell power (Balance).

  • Bear: agility and strength are about equal; attack power is half a strength, since strength gives the bear 2. Crit is worth most: 1% is as much as 15–18 strength, as a Swipe crit is more damage and Blood Frenzy's rage for the next Swipe. Agility is mostly its crit. The bear never dies here, so stamina and armour buy almost nothing.
  • Cat: agility first, as it also gives 1 attack power. Crit is worth about half what it is on the bear (one target, no rage behind it). Armour counts at face value (Cat Form does not multiply it) and shortens the rests. Stamina matters at 20–39, where the cat sometimes dies: there it is worth more than strength.
  • Balance to 39: one target at a time, so spell power is everything; intellect and stamina are next to worthless (the rests are short).
  • Balance from 40: the storm runs on mana, so intellect is worth 1.5–2× spell power and spirit about as much; spell power itself adds little (the storm's coefficient is small). At 40–43, right after the respec, Hurricane on three dies often: stamina is worth most there, and intellect's value is noise.

About these numbers

Simulated on client 1.60.1.70753 (the launch talent tree) with the numbers of the 2026-11-03 patch notes. High Order skyborne (Wind Blessed +2% haste, Read Leyline +50% regeneration while eating and drinking). Mobs of your own level; 55% of a level's XP comes from kills. Two gear sets, never swapped: feral (melee greens, two-handed weapon) or Balance (spell power greens and weapon). Hours include walking (Cat Form and Feral Swiftness from 20, Travel Form from 30, mount from 40). Every level is measured with its build's best rotation, over 24 seeds of 30-minute sessions.

Summary

Balance: 81.3 hours (one target to 39, respec at 40, then Hurricane). Feral: 89.6 hours (caster to 12, bear 13–19, cat 20–33, bear from 34; one build, no respec). Cat build: 89.0 hours (bear 13–19 before Cat Form, cat from 20; no respec).

  • Balance is the fastest single route, by 8 hours. The feral and the cat build are level within noise (0.6%).
  • Fastest overall: cat to 39, then a respec to Balance at 40 with spell power gear and Hurricane on three mobs: about 79.1 hours (put together from the two routes' levels).
  • Pulls: the bear and Hurricane pull three mobs at a time, but 7.5% of their kills are single-mob pulls (mobs standing alone), fought with the single-target rotation. The cat and Balance before 40 always pull one.
  • Not tried in game: whether three mobs stay inside Hurricane, and the extra health the patch notes give higher-level mobs (no number published, not modelled). Both could change the result.
  • What does not pay while levelling: openers from stealth (no chain pulls while prowling), Starfire as the filler, Shifting Power (its mana is drunk back between pulls).
  • How a route is made: a talent order written by hand from the tooltips and from where the time goes; the sim checks it. Each level fights in whichever form is fastest there.
  • Death: 2 minutes back to the corpse and healed to full; the XP of the mobs being pulled is lost.
  • Gear: a typical green set of each level (fitted on the real greens to level 30, then scaled with item level), worn two levels late, and a guide's blue weapons.
  • Not simulated: Bash, Tranquility.
